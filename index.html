Our Story

A California & Connecticut Journey

From New England autumns and Yale in Connecticut to California redwoods, the Pacific coast, and our favorite shared adventures.

The Proposal Ancient Redwoods • California

Our journey weaves together the vibrant coastlines of Northern California and the timeless beauty of Connecticut and New England. From Yeshwanth's Yale PhD in New Haven and colorful autumn road trips across Maine, New Hampshire, and Vermont, to tranquil walks among ancient coastal giants in Humboldt Redwoods State Park—where Yeshwanth proposed beneath the towering redwoods—and sunsets across the Golden Gate.

Along the way, our story is celebrated through the simple moments we treasure most: exploring scenic mountain and ocean trails on weekend hikes, embarking on spontaneous road trips, savoring bubbling Korean soft tofu, rich bowls of ramen, fresh sushi, and leisurely weekend brunches, and sharing cozy evenings connecting over intricate jigsaw puzzles and competitive board game nights.

Together with our families, we honor our sacred cultural roots while celebrating our shared life across both coasts. We are blessed to have you in our lives and cannot wait to take our sacred steps together amidst the golden hills of Stemple Creek Ranch.

Telugu Wedding Methodology

Wedding Ceremony

A guided walk through the sacred rites conducted in Sanskrit at the Kalyana Mandapam.

The Kalyana Mandapam (కల్యాణ మండపం)

The Wedding takes place in the Kalyana Mandapam. This sacred wedding pavilion symbolizes the Universe. The wedding ceremony is conducted in Sanskrit, the language of the Hindu Scriptures, following timeless traditions.

Step 1 10 minutes
గణపతి పూజ
Ganapathi Puja

Lord Maha Ganapathi is invoked to preside over the wedding to ward off any obstacles to the ceremonies and bless the union with auspicious beginnings.

Step 2 10 minutes
పుణ్యాహవాచనం
Punyaha Vachana

The Priest proclaims the date, time, and location of the wedding in the cosmic era as sacred. He invokes God to purify the wedding location and to bless the bride, bridegroom, their families, friends, and all assembled for this holy occasion.

Step 3 15 minutes
గౌరీ పూజ & వర పూజ
Gowri Puja & Vara Puja

Gowri Puja (10 mins): Goddess Gowri, consort of Lord Shiva, is worshipped to bless the Bride with an auspicious, happy, prosperous, fruitful, and long married life, along with ideal progeny.

Vara Puja (5 mins): The Bride’s father welcomes and brings the groom to the Kalyana Mantapa for the wedding.

Step 4 10 minutes
కన్యాదానం
Kanyadanam

The Bride is made to sit in a cane weaved basket (symbolizing her father’s lap) and is given as a sacred gift to the bridegroom through her parents with solemn blessings.

Step 5 5 minutes
పాణిగ్రహణం
Panigrahanam

This means “holding hands”. The groom tenderly holds the hands of the bride. The Mantras say: “The Devas have offered you to me in order that I may live the life of a householder (Grihasta). We shall not part from each other even after we grow old!”

Step 6 • Sumuhurtham 10 minutes
శుభ ముహూర్తం (జీలకర్ర & బెల్లం)
Subha Muhurtham (Jeera & Jaggery)

The sacred paste of Jeera (cumin seeds) and Jaggery is placed over each other's heads at the exact auspicious moment. That creates an eternal tie of affinity and affection, happiness, and mutual devotion to each other.

Step 7 • Sacred Thali 10 minutes
మాంగళ్యధారణం & సింధూరం
Mangalya Dharana (Sindhur also)

Timed to the exact auspicious hour is the tying of the Mangala Sutra (Thali). The golden symbol of wedding is the Mangalya, strewn on a golden chain with black beads. The Priest requests all the elders to bless the Mangalya. The groom ties the Mangalya around the bride's neck with three sacred knots. Sindhur is also applied to her forehead.

Step 8 10 minutes
తలంబ్రాలు (అక్షతారోపణం)
Talambralu (Akshata Aropana)

The Couple hold a handful of rice and pour it on each other’s head to wish each other success and prosperity. Just as rice is the only plant that is planted in one place and replanted in another place after it grows to a certain stage, in this ceremony, it is used to symbolize the change in this stage in the bride’s life.

Step 9 5 minutes
పూలదండలు & ఉంగరాల మార్పిడి
Exchange of Garlands & Gold Rings

The exchange of floral garlands symbolizes their one soul in two bodies—the inward acceptance by each of the other's very fragrance. The bride and the groom then exchange gold wedding rings.

Step 10 10 minutes
ప్రధాన హోమం (హవన్)
Havan / Pradhana Homam

Homa is usually a witness of marriage. The Agni hotra protects them at all times. This sacrificial fire signifies all deities’ divine blessings upon the newlyweds.

Step 11 • Seven Sacred Vows 10 minutes
సప్తపది (ఏడు అడుగులు)
Sapthapadi (The Seven Steps & Vows)

The priest ties the sacred nuptial knot joining the couple's garments. Taking seven sacred steps together around the Agni Hotra, they seal their eternal union with seven vows:

1. Nourishment & Home Responsibilities
2. Strength, Courage & Protection
3. Prosperity & Sharing Wealth
4. Joy, Peace & Spiritual Values
5. Virtuous Children & Lineage
6. Lifelong Companionship & Harmony
7. Eternal Love, Health & Devotion
Step 12 • Concluding Blessings 15 minutes
హారతి, ఆశీర్వచనం & అరుంధతీ దర్శనం
Harathi, Ashirvachanam & Arundhati Darshanam

The wedding ceremonies culminate in three sacred concluding blessings:

హారతి (Harathi): Turmeric and kumkum flame offering to ward off negative energies and invoke divine auspiciousness.
ఆశీర్వచనం (Ashirvachanam): Priests, elders, and all guests shower the couple with yellow sacred rice (అక్షతలు / Akshantalu) and flowers.
అరుంధతీ దర్శనం (Arundhati Darshanam): The newlyweds salute the Sun and gaze toward the pious star Arundhati, pledging lifelong fidelity and devotion.
